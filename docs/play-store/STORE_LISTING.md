## Full description
```
EnCaisse, ensemble.

EnCaisse is a shared group cash (caisse) app for roommates, families, and friends who need one clear place for money in and money out.

Create a group or join with an invite code. Add deposits and expenses with categories, optional receipt photos, and a full history. Everyone in the group sees the same live balance — always calculated from movements, never a stale stored total.

What you can do
• Sign in with email or Google
• Create or join a shared caisse
• Log deposits and expenses with categories
• Attach optional receipt photos
• Soft-delete with a short undo window
• Get notified when members add activity
• Use the app in many languages (EN, FR, AR, ES, PT, ZH, HI, RU, JA)
• Light or dark theme

Built for clarity: flat design, teal & coral branding, and a balance that stays honest.

EnCaisse — shared cash, shared peace of mind.
```
