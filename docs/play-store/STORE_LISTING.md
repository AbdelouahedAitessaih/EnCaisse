## English (en-US)

### Short description (78 / 80)
```
Shared group cash: split expenses, track who owes whom and settle up together.
```

### Full description
```
EnCaisse, ensemble.

One shared caisse for trips, roommates, families and friends. Everyone sees the same live balance, every expense is split fairly, and you always know who owes whom.

SPLIT ANY EXPENSE
• Split equally between the people involved, or set custom amounts
• Pay in another currency: enter the amount in EUR, USD… and it is converted into the caisse currency at the rate you set
• Recurring expenses and deposits (weekly or monthly) are added for you

KNOW WHO OWES WHOM
• A clear "who owes whom" list with the fewest payments possible
• Record a payment in one tap; the person who receives it confirms it
• Spending by category with charts

EVERYONE ON THE SAME PAGE
• Join with a 6-character code or an invite link
• Live balance, always calculated from the movements — never a stale total
• Notifications when members add activity
• Append-only audit trail: every change is kept, nothing silently disappears
• Deleted by mistake? Undo it

YOUR OWN CAISSE TOO
• A personal caisse to track your own money next to your groups
• Search and filter movements by type, category, member or date
• Attach receipt photos
• Export to CSV or PDF

PRIVATE AND SECURE
• Sign in with email or Google
• Optional app lock with fingerprint, face or device PIN
• Home-screen widget with your balances
• Works offline and syncs when you are back online

9 languages: English, Français, العربية, Español, Português, 中文, हिन्दी, Русский, 日本語. Light and dark themes.

EnCaisse — shared cash, shared peace of mind.
```

Upload the six screenshots in file order — the first three are what most
people see without scrolling. Delete the old screenshots from the listing.

To regenerate (after a UI change): seed demo data with
`node scripts/store-demo.cjs seed`, join the printed code from a phone,
`node scripts/store-demo.cjs fill <your-email>`, capture the six screens into
`docs/images/store-captures/<locale>/` (build with
`--dart-define=HIDE_ADS=true`), run `python tools/frame_store_screenshots.py`,
then `node scripts/store-demo.cjs cleanup`.
