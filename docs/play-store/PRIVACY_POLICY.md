# Privacy Policy — EnCaisse

**Last updated:** 11 August 2026  
**App:** EnCaisse (`com.encaisse.encaisse`)  
**Contact:** Replace with your support email before publishing (e.g. `support@yourdomain.com`)

This Privacy Policy describes how EnCaisse (“we”, “the app”) collects, uses, and shares information when you use our mobile application.

## 1. Who we are

EnCaisse is a shared group cash (“caisse”) app that helps people track deposits, expenses, and a live group balance.

## 2. Information we collect

### Account information
- Name, email address, and authentication identifiers when you sign up with email/password or Google Sign-In
- Optional profile photo if you choose to upload one

### Group and financial activity data
- Groups you create or join, invite codes, membership roles
- Movements (deposits and expenses): amounts, titles, categories, dates, optional receipt images
- Audit / activity history related to those movements

### Device and notifications
- Firebase Cloud Messaging (FCM) token to deliver push / local notifications (if you allow notifications)
- App settings stored on device (language, theme preferences)

### Advertising identifiers
- Android Advertising ID (AD_ID) may be used by Google AdMob to show ads and measure ad performance

### Photos / media
- Access to photos or camera only when you choose to attach a receipt or change a profile / group photo

We do **not** sell your personal information.

## 3. How we use information

- Provide and operate the app (accounts, groups, balances, sync)
- Authenticate you and secure your account
- Send in-app / push notifications about group activity (when enabled)
- Show advertisements via Google AdMob
- Improve reliability and fix issues (including crash / diagnostic data processed by Google Firebase / Google Play services where applicable)

## 4. Legal bases (where applicable)

Depending on your region, we process data to perform our contract with you (providing the service), with your consent (e.g. notifications, optional photos), and for legitimate interests such as security and fraud prevention.

## 5. Sharing of information

We share data with service providers that help us run EnCaisse:

| Provider | Purpose |
|----------|---------|
| Google Firebase (Auth, Firestore, Storage, Cloud Messaging) | Account, database, file storage, notifications |
| Google Sign-In | Optional authentication |
| Google AdMob / Google advertising services | Ads and ad measurement |
| Google Play | Distribution and related platform services |

Group members you invite can see shared group content (movements, balances, membership information relevant to that group).

We may disclose information if required by law or to protect rights, safety, and security.

## 6. Data retention

- Account and group data are kept while your account / groups remain active
- Soft-deleted movements may be retained for a limited undo window, then removed according to app logic
- You may request deletion of your account and associated personal data by contacting us (see Contact). Group data shared with others may remain where still needed for other members until the group is deleted by the creator

## 7. Security

We use industry-standard providers (Firebase / Google) and access controls. No method of transmission or storage is 100% secure.

## 8. Children’s privacy

EnCaisse is not directed to children under 13 (or the minimum age required in your country). We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

## 9. International transfers

Your data may be processed on servers operated by Google in various countries. Where required, appropriate safeguards apply under Google’s terms and compliance programs.

## 10. Your rights

Depending on your location, you may have rights to access, correct, delete, or export your personal data, or to object to / restrict certain processing. Contact us to exercise these rights.

You can also:
- Update profile information in the app
- Revoke Google Sign-In access from your Google account settings
- Disable notifications in system / app settings
- Uninstall the app to stop local processing

## 11. Ads

The app may show ads via Google AdMob. Ad providers may use cookies or advertising IDs as described in Google’s policies:  
https://policies.google.com/technologies/ads

We may update this Privacy Policy. We will revise the “Last updated” date and, where appropriate, notify you in the app or store listing.
