Privacy Policy — EnCaisse

Last updated: 11 August 2026
App: EnCaisse (com.encaisse.encaisse)

This Privacy Policy describes how EnCaisse (“we”, “the app”) collects, uses, and shares information when you use our mobile application.

1. Who we are

EnCaisse is a shared group cash (“caisse”) app that helps people track deposits, expenses, and a live group balance.

2. Information we collect

Account information: name, email, authentication identifiers (email/password or Google Sign-In), optional profile photo.

Group and activity data: groups, invite codes, memberships, movements (amounts, titles, categories, dates), optional receipt images, audit history.

Device / notifications: FCM token (if notifications allowed), on-device settings (language, theme).

Advertising: Android Advertising ID may be used by Google AdMob.

Photos: accessed only when you attach a receipt or change a photo.

We do not sell your personal information.

3. How we use information

  • Provide accounts, groups, balances, and sync
  • Authenticate and secure your account
  • Send notifications about group activity (when enabled)
  • Show ads via Google AdMob
  • Improve reliability (including diagnostics via Google services where applicable)

5. Retention & security

Data is kept while your account/groups are active. Soft-deleted movements may be retained briefly for undo. We use industry-standard providers; no system is 100% secure.

6. Children

EnCaisse is not directed to children under 13 (or the minimum age in your country).

7. Your rights

You may request access, correction, or deletion of personal data by contacting us. You can also update your profile in-app, revoke Google access, disable notifications, or uninstall the app.

8. Ads

Ads may be shown via AdMob. See Google’s advertising policies.
