Delete your EnCaisse account

App: EnCaisse (com.encaisse.encaisse)
Last updated: 11 August 2026

You can request deletion of your EnCaisse account and associated personal data at any time.

How to request deletion

  1. Send an email to aitsaihabdelouahed@gmail.com.
  2. Use the subject line: EnCaisse account deletion request.
  3. Include the email address used to sign in to EnCaisse (email/password or Google).
  4. Optionally include your display name and that you want your account and associated personal data deleted.
What we delete

After verifying ownership of the account, we delete your user profile and personal account data. We aim to complete deletion within 30 days.

What may remain

  • Shared group history (movements you created) may remain visible to other group members until the group creator deletes the group, because that data is part of the shared caisse record.
  • We may keep limited records if required by law or for security / fraud prevention.

Before you email

If you are the creator of a group and want all shared data removed, delete the group in the app first (Manage group → Delete group), then request account deletion.
